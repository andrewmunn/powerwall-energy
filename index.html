How this is calculated

Every 30 minutes of Powerwall history is priced at the rate plan saved in your Tesla app: PG&E Residential Time-of-Use Electric Home, NEM 3.0 (2025). Grid purchases and the no-solar comparison use the buy price for that season and hour. Power sent to the grid earns that month's NEM 3.0 export rate for the hour, which is a few cents at midday and higher in the evening. The same 2025 rates are used for every month. Fixed monthly charges, taxes and fees aren't included, so your real bill is higher than "Paid" by those amounts. The Tesla app reports bigger savings because it appears to credit exports at close to the retail price. "Without Powerwall" replays the same solar output and home use with no battery: solar powers the home as it's made, the surplus is exported and the rest is bought. It assumes the panels would produce the same energy through a separate solar inverter, since the Powerwall 3 also serves as yours.
